# Delete your Brew-ha account

**In the app:** Profile → Edit Profile → Delete my account.

**By email:** send a message from the email address on your account to
[info.brewha@gmail.com](mailto:info.brewha@gmail.com) with the subject **"Delete my account."**
We'll complete it within 7 days.

## What's deleted

Your profile, photo, payment receipts, stamps and unused vouchers.

## What's kept

Past order records, without your name or contact number, for the café's business records.
