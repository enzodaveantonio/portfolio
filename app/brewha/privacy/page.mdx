# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

## Privacy Policy

Brew-ha Cafe & Bar collects only what it needs to take your orders, verify payments and run the loyalty program. We do not sell your data. This policy follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

### 1. What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email and password | You sign up with email | To sign you in. Passwords are stored encrypted; we cannot see them. |
| Name, email and profile photo from Google | You sign in with Google | To create and sign in to your account |
| Name and contact number | Sign-up, checkout, profile edits | To identify your order at pickup and contact you about it |
| Profile photo | You choose to upload one | Shown on your profile |
| Order details: items, options, notes, totals, times | Every order, including guest orders | To prepare your order and keep business records |
| Payment receipt screenshots | You pay by bank or e-wallet transfer | To verify your payment. Receipts may show your name and account details. |
| Stamps and vouchers | Completed orders | To run the loyalty program |
| Push notification token | You allow notifications | To send order updates to your phone |

We do not collect card numbers, your location, or your contacts. The app uses your camera or photos only when you choose to upload a photo or receipt.

We do not collect card numbers, your location, or your contacts. The app uses your camera or photos only when you choose to upload a photo or receipt.

### 2. Who can see it

- **You:** your own profile, orders, receipts, stamps and vouchers.
- **Authorized café staff:** orders, contact details and payment receipts, to prepare orders and verify payments.
- **Service providers** that run the app for us, only as needed: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Expo (delivery of notifications) and Google (Google sign-in and Android notifications). Their servers may be outside the Philippines.
- **Authorities,** only when the law requires it.

### 3. How we protect it

Access rules in our database limit each customer to their own data. Payment receipts are kept in private storage and opened by staff only through links that expire within minutes.

### 4. How long we keep it

- Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
- Payment receipt screenshots: 1 year after the order.
- Order records: as long as needed for business and tax records. After you delete your account, these are kept without your name or contact number.

### 5. Deleting your account

Go to **Profile → Edit Profile → Delete my account** in the app, or email info.brewha@gmail.com from the address on your account. Email requests are completed within 7 days.

Deleting your account removes your profile, photo, notification tokens, payment receipts, stamps and unused vouchers. Unused vouchers cannot be restored. Order records stay in our accounts without your personal details.

### 6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to its processing, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. Email us to use any of these. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission ([privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph)).

### 7. Notifications

We send order updates only if you allow notifications. You can turn them off anytime in your phone's settings.

### 8. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
